# ADR Lifecycle

## Purpose
Defines how ADRs evolve over time.

---

## Status Flow

Proposed → Accepted → Deprecated → Superseded

---

## Definitions

### Proposed
Under discussion

### Accepted
Approved and implemented

### Deprecated
No longer recommended

### Superseded
Replaced by another ADR

---

## Update Rules

- Do NOT edit accepted ADRs
- Create a new ADR for changes
- Link to previous ADRs

---

## Review Frequency

- Major systems: quarterly
- Critical decisions: after major incidents
